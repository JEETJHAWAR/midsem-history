Essays (15 marks)
  1. Was Marxist "new history" an advance over colonial and nationalist history? (S4) (model essay given in class)
  2. Europe transformed but did not create Indian Ocean connectivity (S7 + S9 + S10 + S11)
  3. "Religion travelled because people travelled" (S9 + S10)
  4. "Unity in the large, disunity in the small": Chaudhuri and Roy (S11)

Essay shape: intro with a thesis → 3–4 paragraphs, each = one point + scholar + example → one "but" paragraph → conclusion linking to "India was connected long before Europe".

5 sentences you can drop into almost any answer
  1. "The past is a permanent dimension of human consciousness" (Hobsbawm): every age rewrites it for its own needs.
  2. Every source is both evidence and interpretation, so historians must corroborate.
  3. The scale we choose (city, region, ocean, globe) decides what we see.
  4. Asia was connected long before 1500; Europe transformed but did not create that world.
  5. Religion travelled because people travelled, and it was translated, not simply copied.
