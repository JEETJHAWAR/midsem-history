# S14 Geoff Wade, "Islam Across the Indian Ocean to 1500 CE" (Schottenhammer ed., Early Global Interconnectivity vol II ch 5, pp85–138). Wade: Australian Parliamentary Library.
- Islam to S & E Asia by land + sea; this = sea "girdle of trade" Arab world → S Asian & SE Asian ports → S China. Less visible than Buddhism/Saivism/Vaishnavism (no iconography). Sources: few texts, shipwreck items (Islamic jewellery moulds, tasbih beads), TOMBSTONES, newly mined CHINESE texts.
- Baghdad founded 762 (Abbasid) + Kufa, Wasit, al-Ubulla, Basra outlet → impetus to Gulf–East Asia trade. Buyids (932–1044): Siraf dominant. Sind ports al-Daybul, al-Mansurah. Hourani: Umayyads (660–749) & Abbasids (750–870) alongside Tang helped long voyages. Routes: Gulf → Kulam-Mali (Quilon) or coast Sind → Sri Lanka → Kalah-bar (Kedah) → Sumatra/Java or Selat (Melaka Straits) → Sanf (Champa) → Luqin → Khanfu (Canton); ~6 months; Ibn Khurdadhbih. 825 Basra navy vs Bahrayn pirates. 878 massacre of foreign merchants in Canton (Huang Chao) ended direct trade → SEGMENTED trade (Arabian Sea / Bay of Bengal / SCS); after Siraf: Kish (late 11th–13th c), then Hormuz (14th–15th). Egyptian route from 10th c (al-Maqdisi 985: Fustat "like the Baghdad of old"); Aden key under Fatimids (979–1175) & Ayyubids (1175–1250); Karimi merchants (tujjar al-Karim) guilds, correspondents in Aden & India — "great mercantile travellers" (Di Meglio).
- Eaton: by late 7th c Muslims saw themselves as carriers of global civilisation; Arabic + Islam = "civilizational coherence". Question: Sufis as spreaders before 1500?
- Wink: outside conquered Makran, Sind, Zabul, Kabul, Punjab no permanent Muslim communities beyond coastal towns before 11th c; on coasts communities "everywhere their raison d'etre was trade".
- GUJARAT/KONKAN: Muslims from Gulf/Oman/Hadramawt under Rashtrakuta "Ballahara" kings; al-Masudi: ~10,000 Muslims at Saymur (Chaul), 4 Friday mosques; king appoints Muslim leader over Muslims; Ibn Hawqal (976): at Qamhul, Sindan, Saymur, Kinbaya "Muslim institutions of law are practiced openly" under infidel rulers; bayasira = Muslims born in India of Muslim parents. Chalukya Siddharaja (1094–1143) fostered coastal Muslim communities (legend: converted by Bohras, Khojas). By 12th c three segments; trade east of India controlled by Indian Muslims; Gujarati merchants (Muslim & Hindu) prominent; 13th c+ Sultanate of Gujarat; hinterland cotton cloth, indigo, saltpetre. Ibn Battuta at Cambay: foreign merchants build mansions & mosques; Honavar Shafi'i women know Qur'an by heart; Malabar paid Sultan Jamal al-Din of Honavar.
- MALABAR: Calicut dominant from 11th c (tomb 780s near Calicut earliest artefact) under Samudri raja/Zamorin; pepper; Shafi'i coastal Muslims tied to Middle East & tropical Shafi'i societies vs inland Hanafi "Tartars"; by 13th c dominant in long-distance commerce under Hindu kings; 15th c only Cannanore & Honavar Muslim-ruled; Ma Huan: chiefs Muslim, 20–30 mosques; Abd al-Razzaq Samarqandi 1442: 2 mosques, qadi, Shafi'i; "town inhabited by infidels... on a hostile shore".
- SRI LANKA: cinnamon; "Moors" claim Arab descent; triangle pepper (Malabar)–cinnamon (SL)–pearls (Coromandel); later destroyed by Portuguese 16th c.
- COROMANDEL (Ma'abar from 12th c): inscription 875 earliest; major after 11th c; conversions 13th c; Rashid al-Din: Ma'abar "key to India", horse purchase from Kish; al-Tayyibi family (rulers of Fars) as ministers/governors of 3 ports; 1280 letter to Yuan: "all the gold, pearls... of Muslim countries come from here"; Rasulid stipend list 1290; Madurai Sultanate 1335–1378 → Vijayanagara.
- CONVERSION: legends (Cera king converted after dream — Mappila tradition; Maldives); Eaton: Ghaznavid raids from 986, temple desecration political not religious (against enemy king's state-deity); Pearson: Shafi'i more relaxed about living under non-Muslims; rush of mosque building 12th c; Levtzion (1979) assimilation not imposition; David Parkin "acceptance of Islam" additive. Pardesi (foreign) vs Mappila (local converts); equality — Gaspar Correia: low-caste Malabaris could move freely once Muslim, outside Nayar law. Coromandel: Marraikkayar, Kayalar (Shafi'i, SEA trade), Rawther, Labbai (Hanafi).
- NETWORKS (India): Ibn Battuta's diversity (Omani qadi Panderani, Bahrayni amir Calicut, Iraqi Shi'a merchant Quilon, Qazvin qadi, Mogadishu jurist Mangalore 4000 Muslims). (1) family/origin — Hadhrami religious authorities in Malabar from 1204; Engseng Ho Graves of Tarim. (2) contracts: mufawada universal partnership (Muslims only; Shaybani Kitab al-Asl, 8th c), commenda (investors→agent). Sufi tariqa from 11th c; merchant & religious specialist often same person; Isami 1350: "every country... under the protection of a fakir". Qadiriyya (Abd al-Qadir Gilani 1077–1166) at Nagore. KAZARUNIYYA: Abu Ishaq Kazaruni (963–1033); hospices (khanqah) at Cambay, Calicut, Quilon, Quanzhou; ships pledged money to the saint; Trimingham: "Kazaruni Sufi insurance company"; suppressed by Safavids. (3) Lambourn KHUTBA network (1290s): Rasulid Aden customs document Nur al-ma'arif lists stipends (dyestuffs & gold) for qadis & khatibs at 40+ places on Indian coast; Rasulid sultan named in Friday sermon = allegiance without territorial rule; Calicut letter asking to name Rasulid sultan; "preferred trading partner". (4) Cambay–Sumatra–Java gravestones.
- SE ASIA three periods: (1) to Chola invasions 11th c; (2) late 11th–13th c; (3) 14th–15th c. Envoys to Northern Song (Kaifeng) from Dashi, Chola, Zabaj (Srivijaya), Champa with Muslim names, "surnamed" Li (= 'Ali; maybe Alid/Shi'a refugees — Dimashqi: Alids fleeing Umayyads & al-Hajjaj settled in Champa) and Pu (= Abu). Kalah/Kra (Guluo) entrepot, tin, "rendezvous of ships of Siraf and Oman... ships of China" near Takuapa/Kedah. Barus/Fansur camphor; 10th–11th c glass seal "Allah Muhammad" (Kufic). 980s Champa Muslims fled to Hainan & Guangzhou (Pu Luo'e 986 to Hainan; Husayn with 300 to Guangzhou 988) → Sanya community. Chola raids 1017/1025: Sanfoqi envoys stop 1028; Dashi missions hiatus 1019–1050s; Li-surname envoys vanish.
- First Muslim rulers: Lamreh gravestone Sultan Sulaiman 1211 (disputed); Marco Polo 1290s Perlak Muslim; Samudera Sultan Malik al-Salih grave 1297; envoys Hasan & Sulaiman 1280s; title al-Malik al-Zahir echoes Mamluk Baybars (Ayn Jalut 1260). Why 13th c? Chola decline, Vijayanagara inward → end of Tamil guild economy → new avenues. Ibn Battuta 1345–46 Samudera: Sultan al-Malik al-Zahir Shafi'i, jihad vs infidels who pay jizya, missions to Delhi & Quanzhou; but batu Aceh show indigenous element. 1326 sultan shahid (martyr); Aru 1411, Lambri 1412 Muslim rulers. Adityawarman retreated to Minangkabau highlands (1356 Bukit Gombak inscription) — maybe fleeing jihad. Java "country of infidels" mid-14th c; Champa princess spoke Turkish, wrote bismillah.
- QUANZHOU 1360s purge ("Isfahan rebellion" 1357–1366: Persian commanders Saifuding & Amiliding vs Pu descendant Nawuna; then Chen Youding + Jin Ji purge Sunnis then all Muslims ~decade) → refugees by sea → Muslim tombs at Trowulan/Tralaya (Majapahit) from 1370s (Saka year + Arabic), Puteri Cempa 1448; Demak 1377; Gresik 1000+ "Chinese" families; Barus tombstone 1370 (woman Suy); Brunei "Maharaja Bruni" gravestone carved in Quanzhou; Terengganu stone 14th c (Malay in Arabic script, "Dewata Mulia Raya" + Allah, fines by rank); Minye Tujuh Arabic + Old Malay. Hybridity.
- Ma Huan (with Zheng He 1413–15, 1421–22, 1431–33; Yingyai shenglan): Java 3 classes — western Muslim merchants, Tang (Chinese from Guangdong, Zhangzhou, Quanzhou, many Muslim), locals ("devil worship"); Malacca king & people Muslim; Aru, Lambri Muslim; Bengal Muslim, Farsi.
- MALACCA: Parameswara (Indic title, envoy Abd Allah Hasan 1408); Megat Iskandar Shah c.1414 (Pires: converted at 72 on marrying daughter of Pasai ruler); Sri Maharaja; 1445 Sri Parameswara Dewa Shah; 1450s Sultan Muzaffar Shah (coins); Islamic rule underwritten by Ming base? Mansur Shah mosque, non-Muslim officials; 1000 Gujaratis in Malacca when Portuguese came. Pahang first sultan d.1475.
- Zheng He (1405–1435) Muslim eunuch admiral; Yongle's legitimacy, pax Ming; up to hundreds of ships, 30,000 troops; 1407 edict protecting Muslims; Ming bases at Malacca & Samudera; Java West king (Majapahit) killed East king 1406 + 170 Ming troops → 60,000 liang gold; Parlindungan "Annals of Semarang and Cirebon": Chinese Hanafi network (Gan Eng Cu = Gong Yongcai 1429 in Ming shilu). Pires: Javanese pesisir pates "descended from Chinese, Parsees and Kling"; Demak's Pate Rodim; 50,000 tapas ascetics (syncretism, agama Jawa); Moluccas Islam 50 yrs ago, Banda 30 yrs.
- Conversion debate: Eaton 2 lines — (a) trade boom 14th–15th c after Mongol invasions + European spice demand → cosmopolitan Straits unified by Islam; (b) intellectual/spiritual networks (Pires: "chiefly Arab mullas"). Feener: quantitatively significant local conversion only end 13th c, among political & mercantile elites. Jihad also.
- SE Asia networks: (1) Alid/Shi'a Li & Pu; (2) Sufi via Sumatra/Yemen — Mas'ud al-Jawi teacher in Aden (pupil al-Yafi'i d.1367), Qadiriyya; (3) Samudera sultans; (4) Quanzhou refugees; (5) Gujarat–archipelago: Lambourn 15 graves at Pasai & Gresik from Cambay (Moquette 1912; Malik Ibrahim d.1419 Gresik; parallels with 'Umar al-Kazaruni 1333 at Cambay; marble maybe recycled from Hindu/Jain temple).
- CHINA: Ibn Khurdadhbih ports Luqin, Khanfu (Guangzhou), Khunju (Quanzhou), Qansu (Yangzhou). 758 Guangzhou sacked by "Dashi". Suleiman 851: Muslim community under qadi confirmed by Chinese. 878/79 Huang Chao killed 120,000 Muslims, Jews, Christians, Parsees (Abu Zaid). Shi'a refugees from Khurasan on river island (al-Marwazi); Ibadi Omani Abu Ubaydah bought aloeswood. 904 head of foreigners Pu Holi (Abu Ali). Champa: "A-luo-he-ji-ba" (Allahu Akbar) at slaughter (Song huiyao). Quanzhou eclipses Guangzhou 2nd half 11th c; mosque 1009/10; earliest gravestone 1173 Husayn al-Hilati; >half foreign trade controlled by Muslims. Pu clan: Pu Kaizong → Pu Shougeng (maritime trade supervisor 1274; sided with Mongols 1276; massacred Song imperial clansmen; Grand Commander of Fujian & Guangdong; 1281 ordered 200 ships). Brunei grave of Song official Pu 1264. Ibn Battuta: merchant from Isfahan, Kazaruni sheikh Burhan al-Din's khanqah. Tombstone NISBAS (Qazwini, Tabrizi, Isfahani, Shirazi, Bukhari, Yamani, Gilani...) mostly IRAN. Semu officials under Yuan.
